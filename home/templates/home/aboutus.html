What is Heritage Craft Heaven?

Heritage Craft Haven is a digital marketplace dedicated to preserving and promoting Myanmar's rich cultural heritage, with a special focus on traditional silver and bronze crafts. Our platform connects artisans who continue to uphold centuries-old craftsmanship with global buyers who appreciate the timeless beauty of these handmade treasures.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Heritage Craft Haven’s mission is to preserve and promote Myanmar’s traditional silver and bronze craftsmanship by creating a sustainable digital marketplace. We are dedicated to empowering local artisans by providing them with global exposure, ensuring fair trade, and fostering a deep appreciation for the cultural heritage embedded in each handcrafted piece. Through our efforts, we aim to protect these endangered art forms while connecting consumers with ethically sourced, unique treasures that tell the story of Myanmar’s rich history.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the leading global platform for Myanmar’s traditional crafts, where every product reflects centuries of artistry and cultural heritage. We aspire to create a future where these traditional crafts are valued not only for their beauty but also for their cultural significance, ensuring that artisans thrive and that Myanmar’s heritage is passed down to future generations. By expanding our marketplace and educating consumers, we envision a world where heritage craftsmanship flourishes in harmony with modern demands.
