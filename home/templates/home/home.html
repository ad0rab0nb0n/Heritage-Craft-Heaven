Heritage Craft Heaven

This project aims to develop an online platform dedicated to showcasing and selling Myanmar's traditional silver and bronze crafts. By bridging the gap between artisans and a global audience, the platform will serve as a one-stop shop for a variety of items varying in price and quality, catering to religious elderly individuals, those unable to shop in person, and collectors of heritage silver and bronze ware. The platform will promote cultural preservation and provide a convenient shopping experience.

Silver Art of Myanmar

Silver art of Myanmar is renowned for its intricate craftsmanship and rich cultural heritage. Artisans skillfully create detailed designs and patterns, often depicting traditional motifs and scenes from Myanmar's history.

Bronze Casting

Bronze casting is an ancient art form that involves creating sculptures and artifacts by pouring molten bronze into molds. This technique, known for its durability and intricate detailing, has been used for centuries to produce everything from statues to functional items.

Read more

SilverWork

Silverwork is a distinguished craft that involves shaping and engraving silver into decorative and functional items. Renowned for its intricate designs and exceptional artistry, silverwork often features elaborate patterns and motifs

Myanmar Coin

The history of Myanmar's coinage reflects the country's rich cultural heritage and its evolving political landscape. Early coinage in Myanmar dates back to the Pyu city-states (circa 1st century AD), where coins were often made of silver and featured Brahmi script.
